Tourism: Description back to ToC

Ontology for representing tourist accommodation and points of interest in a municipality, reusing classes and properties from the SEGITTUR ontology. It covers accommodation establishments (hotels, guesthouses, aparthotels, holiday rentals) with their category, units and location, as well as historical and cultural resources. The repository examples instantiate accommodation establishments (estur:AccomodationEstablishment and its subclasses Hotel, Hostal, Hostel, Aparthotel and Vacation Rentals) with their rating (accomodationRating), number of units and beds, registration number, description, location and contact point, reusing the classes and properties of the SEGITTUR tourism ontology.
